Where the first
collection begins.

A working shortlist for a girls’ collection, ages 5–13: finished wholesale inventory for speed, plus a small group of private-label partners for signature Faith & Eve pieces.

70%

Curated wholesale

Coats, corduroy bottoms, velvet dresses, shirts, skirts and practical layers. Buy shallow size runs and learn what customers choose.

30%

Faith & Eve originals

Begin with one recognizable sweater, one cardigan and a coordinated hat/scarf—enough to establish authorship without funding twelve factory programs.

Opening budget

$2k–$5k

Use marketplace minimums to spread risk across silhouettes and sizes. Reserve part of the budget for samples, freight, packaging and returns.

Before placing an order

Verify the garment,
not just the photograph.

Request samples and check fiber content, seam finish, shrinkage, pilling and colorfastness. Confirm that every size from 5 through 13 is genuinely available rather than extrapolated from toddler sizing.

Children’s products require careful compliance review. Ask for test reports and written confirmation covering the destination market, labeling, flammability, drawstrings, small parts and chemical restrictions.

Wholesale prices, seasonal availability and minimums change often. “Quote required” is intentional: never build a purchase plan from a marketplace thumbnail or an unverified directory claim.
