Before the first order

Sample first.
Buy with care.

  1. 01

    Check the hand feel. Order samples for fabric weight, seams, shrinkage, pilling, and colorfastness.

  2. 02

    Confirm the size run. Verify true availability from ages 5–13 and ask for garment measurements.

  3. 03

    Price the landed unit. Include freight, duty, packaging, payment fees, and expected returns.

  4. 04

    Request compliance records. Review labeling, flammability, chemical, drawstring, and small-parts requirements.
